Frequently asked questions

If your question isn't here, reach out directly.

What is WISCr, exactly?+
The Winter School in Soft & Continuum Robotics — a one-day open symposium (6 December 2026) followed by a five-day hands-on school (7–11 December 2026), hosted by the IIT Gandhinagar Robotics group. It's the inaugural edition.
Who can attend the symposium vs. the school?+
The Day 0 symposium is open registration — about 75 seats across students, faculty/scientists, and industry. The five-day school is selective: only 25 students are chosen by application, and the school includes symposium access.
What does it cost?+
Students attending both the symposium and school: ₹5,000. Students attending the symposium only: ₹1,000. Faculty and scientists from research labs: ₹3,000. Industry: ₹5,000. All figures exclude GST — see the full breakdown on the Apply page.
I've never touched soft robotics — can I still apply?+
Yes. Monday is built for exactly this: why soft robots, bio-inspiration and embodiment, and hands-on tinkering with in-house robots, plus ROS and SOFA setup, before anything more technical is introduced.
What will I actually build?+
In teams of five, you'll take an open problem on a soft robot that already exists in the IITGN lab from design and BoM (Tuesday) through applied geometry, sensing and control in SOFA (Wednesday–Thursday) to a demonstration and review on Friday.
Are the dates confirmed?+
Yes — 6 December 2026 for the symposium, 7–11 December 2026 for the school. What's still being finalised is the named speaker and mentor list, and the exact application timeline.
Is there support if I can't afford to travel or attend?+
This is being worked out as part of the budget. If cost is a barrier, mention it in your application or reach out directly — we'd rather know before selection than lose a strong applicant to it.
What happens after the school ends?+
WISCr is explicitly designed as a recruitment pipeline for well-prepared MTech and PhD candidates. Strong participants can expect a real conversation about joining the group, on top of whatever they take home from the week itself.
Is this only for people who want a robotics PhD?+
No. It's built as genuine hands-on exposure to soft robotics first — modelling, simulation and build — and a pipeline into the group second. Whether or not research is your path, you'll leave having actually built something.
