Inaugural edition · 2026

Winter School in Soft and Continuum Robotics

There is growing interest in soft robotics within and outside India. needs a platform: somewhere to network with other soft roboticists, and somewhere to train the next generation. WISCr is that platform — a one-day open symposium at IIT Gandhinagar, followed by a five-day hands-on school where 25 selected students, in five teams, work on open problems using soft robots built in-house.

Why WISCr exists

A national platform, and a real pipeline

WISCr is a great opportunity to showcase the soft robotics work of the IITGN robotics group to a national audience; to give students real hands-on experience — modelling, simulation and build, not a lecture series; to build a recruitment pipeline of well-prepared MTech and PhD candidates for the group; and to establish IITGN as the convening point for the Indian soft robotics community, with real media visibility.

National showcase

A symposium that puts IITGN's soft robotics work in front of the country's roboticists.

Real hands-on work

Modelling, simulation and build on in-house robots — not a week of lectures.

A recruitment pipeline

Well-prepared MTech and PhD candidates, found before they apply anywhere else.

A convening point

Establishing IITGN as where the Indian soft robotics community meets.

The shape of the week

One symposium day, five school days

Day 0 brings national speakers to campus. Days 1–5 belong to the 25 selected students — mornings of theory, every session pointed at the team project.

D0

Symposium — Sunday 6 December

Invited talks, a panel and lab tours, closing with a dinner for guests and participants. Open to about 75 registrants: students, faculty, scientists and industry.

D1-D5

Hands-on sessions

From appreaciating biology to designing and analyzing your own soft robot.
